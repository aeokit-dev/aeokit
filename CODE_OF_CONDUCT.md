# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people with different
backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code without attacking
people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or publish another
person's private information.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces where someone represents the aeokit project.

## Enforcement

For public concerns, notify a maintainer in the relevant thread. For a private
conduct report, use [GitHub private vulnerability
reporting](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeokit/security/advisories/new) and put
`Code of conduct` in the title. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or
lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Retaliation against someone
who makes a good-faith report is prohibited.
